DrinkFlow and VendorFlow policy

DrinkFlow and VendorFlow are Studio Samantha products built to help small shops, drink counters, and event vendors organize real work. VendorFlow connects with Square® and does not replace Square.

Data Use

Operational data such as order flow, drink counts, pickup timing, reports, and signup details are used to run the product and support the shop that provided them. Customer-facing screenshots, quotes, or case-study notes should only be used with permission.

Ownership

Studio Samantha owns the product code, layouts, copy, reporting logic, deployment setup, and related technical materials unless a separate written agreement says otherwise. A shop keeps ownership of its own business data, brand assets, and approved materials.
