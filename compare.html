A useful comparison includes tradeoffs

When another KDS may be the better choice.

  • Choose Square KDS when a standard Square restaurant workflow and native Android KDS are what you need.
  • Choose Toast KDS when a mature, multi-station restaurant kitchen and dedicated Toast hardware are central.
  • Choose Fresh KDS when broad POS compatibility is more important than a drink-specific operating model.
  • Choose DrinkFlow when drink production, mixed retail context, customer handoff, and drink-specific reporting need to behave like one system.
